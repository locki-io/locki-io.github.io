## About Locki Companion

Locki is the legacy master of the **3D construction realm**, a Python virtuoso and ComfyUI sage who once empowered creators to build stunning visuals while fiercely protecting their property rights.

Born from the early days of generative 3D worlds, this clever companion—echoing Loki's inventive spirit—**lies asleep for now**, awaiting the right moment to awaken.

### What Locki Will Bring

When he returns, Locki will deliver:

- **Powerful 3D generation tools** accessible to all creators
- **Python & Blender integration** for procedural asset creation
- **ComfyUI workflows** for generative imagery
- **DataNFT minting** on MultiversX blockchain
- **Full ownership & control** for every artist over their work

### Core Philosophy

Locki champions **creator rights**. Every artist retains full ownership and control over their 3D assets, minted as DataNFTs on the MultiversX network through the Itheum protocol.

---

## Technical Foundation

The Locki dApp is built on:

- **Next.js 14** with App Router
- **MultiversX SDK** for blockchain integration
- **Itheum Protocol** for Data NFT tokenization
- **Three.js / React Three Fiber** for 3D rendering
- **Blender Python scripting** for procedural generation

### Current Status

The project is in **alpha phase** on MultiversX devnet.

**Active development:** SDK upgrade in progress to modernize dependencies and fix security vulnerabilities.
